# Whale-watching boats in the Salish Sea

Compiled 2026-10-17 for /ships. **Company list:** the Pacific Whale Watch Association [members page](https://pacificwhalewatchassociation.com/members) (28 members: 16 US, 12 BC). **Boats:** matched by the company that holds each boat's registration.

- **US boats:** the holder of the boat's FCC ship radio licence (weekly FCC file of 2026-09-20). The FCC records the radio licence holder, which is normally the operating company but is not stated to be the owner.
- **BC boats:** the registered owner in Transport Canada's Vessel Registration Query System (searched 2026-10-17).
- **Last seen in AIS:** the newest month of our own AIS-based ship records that has this boat, linked to its /ships card (our data currently ends 2026-09-30).

Not on this list: whale-watch companies outside the association, and boats run by the association's members but registered under a name we could not connect to them.

## Summary

- **US:** 36 boats for 15 of 16 member companies. Not found: Anacortes Whale Watching Tours.
- **BC, named boats:** 17 boats for 6 of 12 member companies, all found in our AIS records.
- **BC, unnamed small boats:** 19 more boats on Transport Canada's small-vessel register, which records no boat names, so they can't be matched to AIS. These are probably the operators' RHIBs and zodiacs.
- **Not found in BC:** Orca Spirit Adventures, Sidney Whale Watching.

- **Spirit of Orca:** the operator is Spirit of Orca, LLC and its site names SPIRIT OF ORCA II ([spiritoforca.com](https://spiritoforca.com)). FCC has no licence under that name. SPIRIT OF ORCA 3 is licensed to Olympic Tours, LLC of Port Townsend, so the link is by boat name only. Our AIS also has a SPIRIT OF ORCA (MMSI 338203383), [last seen 2025-07-29](https://earthatlas.org/ships?v=ef011de4-3ad7-44fe-bf30-612dbfa5c143).
